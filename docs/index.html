Logic bug or just noise?

You edit a quantum circuit and its output changes. Did you break the logic, or is the hardware simply noisier now? A normal test cannot tell, because every quantum measurement is random and every real device is noisy. This page shows how to separate the two by looking at the same circuit twice.
